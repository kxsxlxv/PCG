# Shared facade atlas experiment
Four High-poly source families supply local floor/bay crops, not whole facade
textures or donor meshes. A common atlas has albedo/normal/ORM and padded cells.
New canonical local metric GIS footprints retain exact roof area, total wall
area, height and holes. The mesh subdivides walls into floor/bay quads; one
stable anchor per vertical stack restricts finish variants by roughness-derived
glass extent and center. Entries and plinths are explicitly authored textures
from the same family palette, not identified donor modules. Party walls remain
blind. This is a new flat-roof diagnostic lane, not the integrated Stage32
geometry/detail pipeline. Source scale and roof UV scale not certified.
