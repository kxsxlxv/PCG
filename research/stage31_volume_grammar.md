# Stage 31 — opt-in volume grammar
Stage 30 remains the baseline. The new mode keeps the exact GIS shell and
floor heights. Depths are generic authored priors in floor-height units,
not learned or identified real series. Two-bay external shafts, larger roof
cores, deterministic whole-stack offers and semantic repaint patches use
the existing renderer and shared PBR bank. Volume proposals precede balcony
budgeting; unsafe projections are rejected by actual detail collision tests.
The canonical PCGF ABI is unchanged; research metadata marks the new variant.
The stress test generates 1024 detailed geometry plans, not 1024 GLB exports.
A matched 16-building Stage 30 versus Stage 31 comparison renders at 1280x960.
Limitations: no authoritative building-part input/stepbacks in this mode,
no unique donor textures, no native SDK performance claims. Roof equipment
may extend above canonical roof height; the authoritative shell is unchanged.

The candidate reuses Stage 28 geometry refinements and Stage 29 equipment
recipes on actual new meshes, with unchanged host IDs and deterministic
output. Both passes enforce their caps; the scale test below intentionally
covers the pre-refinement grammar and not the full PBR postprocessors.
