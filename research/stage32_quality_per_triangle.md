# Stage 32 — quality per triangle
Opt-in matched experiment, Stage 31 versus Stage 32. Same canonical plans,
GIS shell, aperture rectangles, projected shafts, external balcony anchors,
cutout rails, and occupancy state. Host-contact backs on balcony slabs and
caps removed after placement. Roof cores reduced to exposed side/top faces
with analytic access door, louver and coping. No contact bottom. All
exposed primary bounds unchanged. No internal loggias.

Stage 28 receives a bounded per-building entry cap allowance (up to 96
triangles), not a mandate to exhaust 1800. Sills and slab drip profiles use
meter-bounded shader masks instead of per-window/apron meshes. Window frame
retrofit and curtain variants use stable existing opening keys; preserve
glass and room-light state. Stage 29 prioritizes roof ventilation, allows up
to 144 additional triangles per building, and retains Lite equipment anchors.

Matched 16-building actual GLB/ABI and 1280x960 EGL comparison, close-up
day/night pairs, shared GLSL mask float readback, 1024 matched base-detail
geometry stress test. Gate >=20% aggregate Near reduction. Visual review
required; no claim of native GPU savings. Stage31 remains opt-in baseline.
Analytic details do not cast independent shadows or change silhouettes.
Private donor maps, authoritative GIS building parts, and native UNIGINE
integration remain outside this experiment.
